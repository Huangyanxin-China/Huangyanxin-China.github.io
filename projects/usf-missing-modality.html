A 3D Unified Diffusion Framework for Multi-Modal Segmentation with Missing Modal

A unified framework combines convolutional features, diffusion modelling and consistency learning for segmentation when MRI modalities are missing.

面向 MRI 模态缺失场景，将卷积特征、扩散建模与一致性学习结合，并通过训练与测试阶段一致的模态补全策略减小分布差异。
