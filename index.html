Yanxin Huang

School of Information and Communication Engineering
University of Electronic Science and Technology of China

My research focuses on medical image segmentation, MRI reconstruction, and radiotherapy target delineation. I study how geometric priors and learning methods can help when contours are sparse, imaging modalities are missing, or acquisitions are undersampled.

我的研究关注医学图像分割、MRI 重建与放疗靶区勾画，探索如何结合几何先验、多模态信息与学习方法，应对稀疏标注、模态缺失和欠采样等问题。

CTV-DGG: Prompt-Conditioned Three-Dimensional Clinical Target Volume Delineation with Geometric Guidance

Yanxin Huang, Jinyu Qiu, Feiyu Chen, Zihao Ren, Yu Liu, Xianliang Wang, Feng Yang, Shun Lu, Shuyuan Zhu

Project overviewResults中文简介

Completes a three-dimensional CTV from sparse contour prompts by learning bounded mixtures of geometric candidates, with exact preservation of the supplied contour planes.

利用少量完整轮廓提示与解剖信息，通过几何候选的有界自适应融合完成三维临床靶区。论文目前处于审稿阶段。
